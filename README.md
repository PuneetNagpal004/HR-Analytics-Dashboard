# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Short Description

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard built with Power BI** to analyze employee attrition and identify patterns across salary, age, education, job role, gender, and years at the company.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX**
* **Excel / CSV**

## 📂 Data Source

HR Employee Attrition Dataset** containing employee information such as age, gender, education, salary, job role, years at company, and attrition.

## ✨ Features & Highlights

* **Business Problem:** HR teams need a clear view of employee attrition and the factors associated with employees leaving the organization.
* **Dashboard Goal:** Analyze attrition patterns and provide an interactive view of key employee and workforce metrics.
* **Key Visuals:** KPIs for total employees, attrition, attrition rate, average salary, average age, and years at company, along with attrition analysis by **education, age, salary, job role, gender, and tenure**.
* **Key Insights:** The dashboard shows **1,470 employees, 237 attritions, and a 16.1% attrition rate**. It also highlights attrition patterns across different salary ranges, age groups, job roles, education categories, gender, and years at company.
* **Business Impact:** Helps HR teams identify employee groups with notable attrition patterns and supports **data-driven workforce analysis and further investigation**.
